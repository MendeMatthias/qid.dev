RELIC FORGE · the last mint studio on BTX L1, by qID. Every relic is a BZA-1 artifact: an 83 byte OP_RETURN slot carrying a 52 byte record whose hash locks an issuer signed engraving on IPFS, plus a carrier coin sent to your address. The coin is the relic, and any treasure rides on that coin. When fork PR #88 activates, minting and transfers end by consensus and relics freeze to their wallets. Opening a treasure chest is an ordinary spend: it works even after the fork, returns the BTX to the holder, and burns the relic. Prices are a tool and engraving fee for a service performed now. A keepsake, not an investment: nothing here promises profit, and after the fork relics cannot be moved or sold on-chain. Official relics verify against the qID issuer registry. Paid orders that cannot be minted before the chain seals are refunded automatically. This page holds no keys and mints nothing. Minting is done by the operator, whose keys never touch the web.
