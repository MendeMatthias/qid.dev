RELIC FORGE · the last mint studio on BTX L1, by qID. Every relic is a BZA-1 artifact: an 83-byte OP_RETURN slot carrying a 52-byte record whose hash locks an issuer-signed engraving on IPFS, plus a 0.001 BTX carrier coin sent to your address — the coin is the relic. When fork PR #88 activates, minting and transfers end by consensus; relics freeze to their wallets forever. Prices are a tool & engraving fee for a service performed now — a keepsake, not an investment; nothing here promises profit, and after the fork relics cannot be moved or sold on-chain. Official relics verify against the qID issuer registry. Paid orders that cannot be minted before the chain seals are refunded automatically. This page holds no keys and mints nothing; minting is done by the operator, whose keys never touch the web.
