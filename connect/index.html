Sign in with a BTX address. No email, no password, no account to make.

How this demo works. In a real app the challenge and the check happen on the site's server, and you sign in your own wallet. Here your browser plays all three, so you can try the exact flow with no wallet installed. The verification is the real qID verifier; nothing is faked. The demo signs with a random throwaway address generated in your browser, never your real wallet.

No wallet? Click the button, then use the demo wallet that appears at the top of the dialog. It signs for you like a real phone would.
