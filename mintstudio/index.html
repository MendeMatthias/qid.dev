BTX Mint Lab · a client-side tool for BTX Artifacts (BZA1). It builds and previews a mint request and proves your address with qID; it holds no keys and mints nothing. Minting is done by the operator, who verifies your proof and signs with keys that never touch this page. Studio artifacts use a dedicated test issuer key, so they verify as valid but unofficial.
